Frequently asked questions

PL-300 or DP-600?

PL-300 focuses on Power BI (prepare, model and visualize data); DP-600 on end-to-end analytics solutions in Microsoft Fabric.

Are there DAX questions?

Yes, PL-300 and DP-600 include DAX, modeling and performance questions.

Are they free?

Yes, you can start for free without signing up.
