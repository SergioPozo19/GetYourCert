SC-900 study guide: what's covered and how to prepare

SC-900 is Microsoft's entry exam for security, compliance and identity. It asks you to configure nothing, but you must know which product solves each need, and the names are very similar.

Concepts (10-15%)

  • Zero Trust and defense in depth
  • Shared responsibility model
  • Encryption, hashing and signing
  • Authentication vs authorization and identity as the perimeter

Microsoft Entra (25-30%)

  • Identity types: users, guests, service principals and managed identities
  • MFA, Windows Hello, passkeys and passwordless sign-in
  • Conditional Access and Identity Protection
  • Governance: access reviews, entitlement management and PIM

Security solutions (35-40%)

  • Azure network protection: DDoS, Firewall, WAF and NSGs
  • Microsoft Defender for Cloud: secure score and workload plans
  • Microsoft Sentinel as SIEM and SOAR
  • Microsoft Defender XDR: Endpoint, Office 365, Identity and Cloud Apps

Compliance solutions (20-25%)

  • Service Trust Portal and Compliance Manager
  • Sensitive information types, sensitivity labels and DLP
  • Retention, records management and eDiscovery
  • Insider risk, audit and Microsoft Purview

2-week study plan

1-2Concepts: Zero Trust, shared responsibility and encryption.
3-6Microsoft Entra: identities, authentication methods, Conditional Access and governance.
7-10Security, the heaviest domain: Defender for Cloud, Sentinel and Defender XDR.

Common exam traps

Defender for Cloud vs Sentinel

Defender for Cloud improves security posture and protects workloads; Sentinel collects signals from many sources to detect, investigate and respond (SIEM and SOAR).

Sensitivity labels vs retention labels

Sensitivity labels classify and protect (encryption, markings); retention labels decide how long content is kept or when it is deleted.

Compliance Manager vs secure score

Compliance Manager measures regulatory compliance with a compliance score; secure score measures the security posture of your configuration.

PIM vs Conditional Access

PIM grants admin roles only when needed and for a limited time; Conditional Access decides whether a sign-in is allowed based on user, device, location or risk.

SC-900 sample questions

Five real questions from our 400-question bank. Try to answer before revealing the solution.

1. Why is identity often called the new security perimeter?

  1. Because firewalls are no longer needed once all users have cloud identities assigned
  2. Because identities replace encryption, so data no longer needs to be protected at rest
  3. Because people and devices access resources from anywhere, so access depends on who asks
  4. Because passwords are never used anymore, so network location is the only remaining control
Show answer

Correct answer: C. With remote work and cloud apps, the network edge no longer defines trust; verifying identity does.

2. Which Microsoft Entra product helps discover, remediate and monitor excessive permissions across multicloud environments?

  1. Entra Domain Services
  2. Entra Verified ID
  3. Microsoft Entra Connect Sync
  4. Microsoft Entra Permissions Management
Show answer

Correct answer: D. Permissions Management is a cloud infrastructure entitlement management (CIEM) solution.

3. Where do security teams manage incidents, alerts, advanced hunting and reports across Defender products?

  1. The Teams admin center
  2. The Exchange admin center
  3. The Microsoft Defender portal
  4. The Azure Cost Management portal
Show answer

Correct answer: C. The Defender portal (security.microsoft.com) unifies incidents and hunting across the Defender suite.

4. Which Defender for Endpoint feature prioritizes software vulnerabilities on devices by exploitability and business impact?

  1. Entra access reviews
  2. Safe Attachments
  3. Customer Lockbox
  4. Defender Vulnerability Management
Show answer

Correct answer: D. Defender Vulnerability Management prioritizes software vulnerabilities by exploitability and business impact, considering threat context and asset value. Safe Attachments, access reviews and Customer Lockbox do not prioritize vulnerabilities.

5. How long does Microsoft Purview Audit (Standard) retain audit records by default?

  1. 180 days
  2. Forever
  3. Seven days, then they move to archive storage
  4. 10 years
Show answer

Correct answer: A. Audit (Standard) keeps records for 180 days; Audit (Premium) extends retention to one year and longer with add-ons.

Frequently asked questions

How many questions does the SC-900 practice exam have?

The question bank has 400 original questions. Each practice exam draws 50 questions weighted by domain, just like the official exam.

What's the passing score for SC-900?

Microsoft's official SC-900 exam requires a score equivalent to 70% to pass. We use the same threshold in our practice exams so you train under realistic conditions.

What domains does the SC-900 exam cover?

Four domains: concepts of security, compliance, and identity (10-15%), capabilities of Microsoft Entra (25-30%), capabilities of Microsoft security solutions (35-40%), and capabilities of Microsoft compliance solutions (20-25%).

Is the SC-900 practice exam free?

Yes, you can practice for free without signing up. Creating a free account adds progress tracking and smart review (SRS); the Pro tier removes daily question limits.

Are these the official Microsoft exam questions?

No. These are original questions written based on Microsoft's official "Skills measured" guide for SC-900, not extracts from the real exam or "exam dumps".

How much does the official SC-900 exam cost?

In the United States it costs 99 USD. Elsewhere the price depends on the country and currency, and you see it when booking from Microsoft Learn. Students may get a discount.

How long is the SC-900 exam and how many questions does it have?

You get about 45 minutes to answer, usually 40 to 60 questions. Besides multiple choice there are yes/no, drag-and-drop and completion items, like in our practice exams.

Can you use Microsoft Learn during the SC-900 exam?

No. Fundamentals exams don't allow Microsoft Learn access; that option only exists in Associate and Expert exams.

Does the SC-900 certification expire?

No. Fundamentals certifications don't expire and don't need renewing.
