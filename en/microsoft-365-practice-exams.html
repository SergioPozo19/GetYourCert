Where to start

Where to start? AB-900 (Copilot and Agent Administration Fundamentals) is the foundation. For administration, AB-650 (Microsoft 365 and AI Services Administrator) is the new associate certification, MD-102 covers devices with Intune and MS-700 covers Teams administration. MS-721 is for Teams Phone, rooms and devices. MS-102 retires on November 30, 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What replaces MS-102?

Microsoft retires MS-102 on November 30, 2026. The new associate Microsoft 365 administration certification is AB-650, which also covers Copilot and agents.

Is there a Copilot practice exam?

Yes: AB-900 covers Copilot and agent administration fundamentals, and AB-650 covers advanced Microsoft 365 and AI services administration.

Are they free?

Yes, you can practice for free without signing up. Pro removes the daily question limit.
