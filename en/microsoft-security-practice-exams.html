Where to start

Where to start? SC-900 (Security, Compliance and Identity Fundamentals) is the starting point. Then pick a specialty: SC-300 for identity and access, SC-200 for security operations (SOC) analysts and SC-500 for cloud and AI security engineering.

Frequently asked questions

Which Microsoft security exam should I take first?

SC-900, which covers security, compliance and identity fundamentals with no prerequisites.

SC-300 or SC-200?

SC-300 focuses on identity and access with Microsoft Entra; SC-200 on detection and response with Defender XDR and Sentinel.
